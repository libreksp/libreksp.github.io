Welcome to the Klondike Software Project!

We stand for free software, and the rights of the end-user.

We plan to have a decently full ecosystem of computer software by the end of the decade. Not GNU-level, but enough to say "I run KSP!".

All of our software will be "libre software", which focuses on *freedom* rather than *free of cost*. 
Ideally, all of our software will be licensed under the planned Klondike Public Licenses.

Feel free to contribute to any of our projects, which are currently hosted on Codeberg! [Contribute!](https://codeberg.org/libreksp)

Later, we will transition off of GitHub Pages, but this is the most ideal solution for now.
